SETTINGS

EMPLOYEE BRIEFING

Welcome to the night shift, watchman. The old display machines in this factory wander after midnight. Keep them out of your booth until 6 AM.

  • Doors — seal the left and right doorways. Click the red panels, or press Q / E.
  • Lights — check the blind spots just outside each door. Click the white panels, or press A / D. The tired bulbs click off on their own after a few seconds.
  • Cameras — click the CAMERA strip or press Space. Switch feeds with the map or keys 1–6.
  • Power — everything you switch on drains the generator. At 0% you are in the dark, and something knows it.
  • Esc or P pauses the shift.

MACHINE DOSSIER

  • Teddy Ragbear — the patchwork mascot. Only creeps while you are buried in the monitors. Favors the east side.
  • Moppet — a rag doll that scurries down the west hall. She freezes when her camera is watched... for a while.
  • Sprocket — a wind-up tin rabbit in the storage bay. Ignore him too long and he sprints. Check CAM 5 to slow his spring.
  • Hollow — an owl that unbolts itself and reappears at either doorway. Listen for the hoot, then check your door lights.
